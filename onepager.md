# Distributor Ordering – one page summary

## What it does

It's a website where our distributors place orders and our sales team looks after them. It shows what's in stock right now and holds the stock as soon as an order is placed. It checks each distributor's credit limit and gives regular buyers points and discounts. Every change to an order is sent to our finance and warehouse system automatically.

## Who uses it

Distributors use it to see products and stock, place and cancel orders, track their orders until delivery, and check their points and tier.

Sales managers see all orders, approve or reject the ones over a distributor's credit limit, and mark orders as dispatched and delivered.

## Points and tiers

A distributor gets 1 point for every ₹100 on a confirmed order. Points from the last 90 days decide the tier: Bronze, Silver (3% off) or Gold (6% off). The discount is added to their next order on its own.

## What it replaces

- Taking orders over phone, WhatsApp and email
- Calling the warehouse to check stock
- Checking credit limits by hand
- Keeping loyalty points in a spreadsheet
- Typing orders again into the finance system

## Why it helps

We stop selling stock we don't have. Most orders are confirmed straight away and only the risky ones need a manager. Distributors can see their rewards, which gives them a reason to order more. And every order has a full history.
