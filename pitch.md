# Distributor Ordering

---

## 1. The problem

Right now distributors order by phone, WhatsApp or email. Orders get missed or typed in wrong.

Nobody can see live stock, so sometimes we promise goods the warehouse doesn't have.

Credit limits are checked by hand, often after the order is already accepted.

Loyalty discounts sit in a spreadsheet, and distributors don't know their tier.

---

## 2. Ordering takes a few minutes

Distributors see every product with the stock that's actually available. Out of stock items show up but can't be ordered.

They can put several products in one order. Their discount is applied for them, and their points and tier are always at the top of the screen.

---

## 3. Every order can be followed

Each order shows where it is, from placed to delivered, and a history of who changed it and when.

If a distributor cancels before dispatch, the stock goes back and any points are taken off automatically.

---

## 4. Sales decides on credit

Orders inside the credit limit are confirmed straight away.

Orders over the limit wait in one list for a manager to approve or reject. Dispatch and delivery are marked from the same screen.

---

## 5. Going live

Integration: connect it to our ERP so finance and the warehouse get every order update. We need to agree the message format with the ERP team. We also need a proper sign-in for each distributor and manager.

Data migration: products and prices, current stock, distributors and their credit limits, and the last 90 days of confirmed orders so everyone starts in the right tier.

Rollout: two week pilot with 3 to 5 distributors, train the sales team, then bring everyone on and pick a date to stop taking phone orders.
